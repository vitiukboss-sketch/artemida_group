Common questions

How much does a click on Google Ads cost?

It depends on the industry and competition, from a few dozen groszy to over ten zloty. In the audit we estimate costs for your keywords.

How is Google Ads different from SEO?

Google Ads brings traffic from day one, but you pay for every click. SEO builds organic traffic more slowly, but without paying per click. They work best together.

Will you take over an existing Google Ads account?

Yes. We start with an account audit, then improve the structure, keywords and conversion tracking.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. The account and data stay with you, and we work on the access you grant us.
