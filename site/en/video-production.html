Common questions

Will you write the script?

Yes. We handle the whole process: script, storyboard, shooting, creator casting, editing and file delivery.

In the formats your campaign needs, most often 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16, ready to publish on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.
