How we work

Four steps we repeat while results keep growing. No long presentations to start with.

  1. 1

    Track

    We look at your ad accounts, analytics and website, and work out how much you can pay for a customer and still profit.

  2. 2

    Aim

    We choose channels and audiences, set a test budget and agree how we will know it works.

  3. 3

    Shoot

    We launch campaigns with several creative versions. We make the videos and graphics ourselves, so launch never waits for assets.

  4. 4

    Adjust

    We move budget to what brings results and switch off what misses. Reports come in plain language.

Common questions

What ad budget do I need to start?

For the first tests, a few dozen zloty a day is usually enough. You pay the ad budget directly to the platform (Meta, Google, TikTok); our fee is separate.

When will I see first results?

We have first data within a few days. Stable results usually come after 2–4 weeks of testing and optimisation.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. The account and data stay with you, and we work on the access you grant us.

Do you make the ad creatives too?

Yes. We have our own video and design production, so creatives are made for the specific campaign and tested in several versions.

Will you take over campaigns that are already running?

Yes. We start with an account audit, then improve the campaign structure, audiences and conversion tracking.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. We agree the scope and length of cooperation in an individual proposal.

Do you only work with businesses in Katowice?

No. Our office is in Katowice, but we work with businesses across Poland.
