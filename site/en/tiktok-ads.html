Common questions

Will TikTok ads work in my industry?

TikTok works well for consumer products, e-commerce, food and local services. In the audit we assess whether it is a good channel for you.

Do I need my own videos?

No. We can write scripts, shoot the videos and pick UGC creators.

What budget do I need to start?

For first tests, a few dozen zloty a day is usually enough. You pay the ad budget directly to TikTok.

Do you also run the account organically?

Yes. We combine organic posts with ads, because the best organic videos usually make the best ads.
