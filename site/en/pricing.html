Common questions

Is the ad budget included in the price?

No. You pay the ad budget directly to the platform (Meta, Google, TikTok). Our fee is a fixed amount plus a percentage of ad spend.

Do you add VAT?

No. We are exempt from VAT under Article 113(1) of the Polish VAT Act, so the amounts shown are final.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. We agree the scope and length of cooperation in an individual proposal.

Where do I start if I already run campaigns?

With an ad audit. We review campaign structure, conversion tracking and budgets, then show you what to fix.
