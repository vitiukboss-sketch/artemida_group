Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026. Version 1.0.

Controller

Artem Vitiuk, Katowice, Poland. Privacy contact: support@artemidagroup.eu.

What we collect

Body data: age, sex, height and weight. Account: your email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash) and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, your name from that account. Weight entries you log. Activity: workouts, GPS routes, times, splits and exercises. Nutrition inputs if you use the meal plan: diet type, foods to avoid. App settings and your consent records.

Health data

Body data, workouts and nutrition inputs are health data. We only process them with your explicit consent, which you give during setup and can withdraw at any time by deleting your data.

Why we use it

To build and adjust your training plan, show your progress, run the GPS tracker and voice cues, and (with Pro) generate AI plans and coach answers. Legal bases: your consent for health data, the contract with you for running the app, and our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure.

Apple Health and Health Connect

If you allow it, the app reads your steps, active calories, exercise minutes, height and weight, and saves your workouts and weight. This data is used only inside the app to show your activity and is never used for advertising or sold.

Location

GPS is used only during a run or walk that you start, including while the screen is locked, until you finish it. It measures distance and pace and draws your route. Location is never tracked outside a workout and never shared for advertising.

Who receives it

Your account, workouts, plans, profile and weight entries are stored on our servers, hosted by Supabase, so they sync between your devices. When you use a Pro AI feature, the data needed for that request is sent to our AI provider to generate the answer. Map tiles for your route are loaded from MapTiler, which sees the map area requested and your IP address. Sign-up and password codes are emailed through Resend. Apple and Google handle sign-in if you choose them. App stores process your payments. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

On your device until you delete it or remove the app. On our servers until you delete it or your account. Deleted data is removed from active systems straight away and from backups within 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your data, withdraw consent, and object to processing. Use Settings > Delete account or write to support@artemidagroup.eu. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will tell you in the app about significant changes to this policy.
